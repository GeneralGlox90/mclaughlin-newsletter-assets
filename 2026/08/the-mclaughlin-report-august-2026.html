A Little Note From Liam
Don’t Let August Slip Away

Hey, homeowners! August is a great month to take care of your investment long before winter comes. As we all know… winter is coming.

Don’t let this August opportunity slip away. Vacations wrap up, school starts creeping back onto the calendar, and before you know it, fall is here.

So this month, I want to keep it practical. If I owned your home, these are the things I’d be looking at before fall:

  • Clean the gutters and check drainage. A small blockage can turn into water problems once the leaves and heavier fall rain arrive.
  • Service the HVAC system. It is better to find a problem now than on the first cold night of the season. If you need a recommendation, let me know!
  • Check windows and exterior doors. Look for worn seals, drafts, sticking, or visible gaps before temperatures drop.
  • Touch up exterior paint and trim. Small areas of peeling paint or exposed wood are easier to address before colder, wetter weather.
  • Handle the repairs you have been putting off. Loose railings, dripping faucets, damaged screens, and other small issues rarely improve with time.
  • Take one honest look at curb appeal. Trim overgrowth, clean the entry, and make sure the outside of your home feels cared for.

None of these projects is especially glamorous, but they can save money, protect the value of your home, and make the transition into fall a lot easier.

And if you are considering a move before the end of the year, August is a good time to start planning. You do not need to have every answer yet. A quick conversation can help you understand what is worth fixing, what can wait, and what buyers are likely to notice.

If you want a straightforward opinion on your home, reach out anytime. I’m always happy to help.

Happening in Northeast Ohio
A few easy reasons to get out this month — family day, date night, or just a good local excuse to enjoy August.
Twins Days Festival
August 7–9 • Twinsburg
The world’s largest annual gathering of twins and multiples, with a parade, contests, entertainment, and a uniquely Northeast Ohio tradition.
Twilight at the Zoo
August 7 • Cleveland Metroparks Zoo
An evening of live music throughout the zoo and one of Cleveland’s signature summer nights out.
Feast of the Assumption
August 13–16 • Cleveland’s Little Italy
Food, music, faith, and neighborhood tradition fill Little Italy for one of the area’s most recognizable summer celebrations.
One World Day
August 30 • Cleveland Cultural Gardens
A free celebration of Cleveland’s many cultures with the Parade of Flags, live performances, family activities, and international food.
