A Little Note From Liam

Hey, homeowners!

Fall has a way of making the home to-do list a little harder to ignore. Before the cold settles in, I wanted to send a few practical reminders, along with some thoughts for anyone considering a move.

There’s also still time to get outside and enjoy it. I’ve included a parks section covering the rest of September and October, plus a fall maintenance checklist you can keep handy.
🏡 Home Update: Budget First. Strategy Always.

Why we’re talking about this: Mortgage rates have climbed recently, and that changes the math. (Remember, housing is not a house problem, IT’S A MATH PROBLEM!)

Buyers face higher monthly payments for the same loan amount. For sellers, it makes realistic pricing and strong marketing even more important.

📈 The rate check: Mortgage News Daily’s national 30-year fixed average reached 7.45% on September 24, 2026, up from 7.26% the day before. Your individual rate will depend on your loan and financial profile.

Source: Mortgage News Daily. Trends through September 24, 2026. Survey averages—not individual loan quotes.

See the latest rates and chart →

Buying? Make the payment work today.

  • Set your comfort zone. You absolutely need to know your budget for your monthly payment. Furthermore, you need to be ready for repairs. Welcome to the world of home ownership 🙄
  • Look for opportunities. A home with less competition or a motivated seller may offer room to negotiate. That may mean a home that has been on the market for a long time. Also, you can stay liquid by requesting seller credits toward closing costs—subject to lender and loan-program limits.
  • Your current loan doesn’t have to be your forever loan. If rates come down and you qualify, refinancing could lower your payment. But buy a house you can afford today—not one that only works if rates fall.

Selling? Price to attract—not chase.

  • Price lower than you think. Not at a fire sale, but certainly lower than you think. Buyers are struggling right now and your mission is to get as many people through the door as possible… and price is the #1 selling point.
  • Make the first impression count. Strong photos, thoughtful preparation and easy showing access matter.
  • Watch the response. Be patient, but use showing activity and buyer feedback to decide whether adjustments are needed.

We’ll use recent comparable sales and your home’s condition to decide what that means for your property.

My take: More feet through the door means more chances to find the right buyer—not a guaranteed bidding war. Pricing and marketing need to work together.

Your Cleveland Metroparks
A little time in the parks, through the end of October.

Our Metroparks are near and dear to me. I’ve been a long time supporter and donor. Constantly there are free ways for adults and families to get outside and enjoy them. If you’re looking for something to do before October wraps up with yourself, a date, or the partner and kids, take a look at whats going on in October:

CanalWay Center • 4524 East 49th Street, Cuyahoga Heights. Most picks below are free; archery has a fee. Check official listings before heading out.
